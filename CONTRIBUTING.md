# Contributing to QC Remote

QC Remote is not currently accepting external code, documentation, artwork,
presets, captures, recordings, or other contributions.

Freevia is the intended publisher and copyright owner, and an open-source
distribution model has been selected. The exact license, Freevia's authority
and chain of title, and the inbound-contribution terms are not yet finalized.
Opening an issue to report a defect does not transfer ownership, but please do
not attach confidential,
copyrighted, trademarked, or personal material that you are not authorized to
share. Pull requests and unsolicited patches may be closed without review or
use until a written contribution policy is adopted.

This interim restriction is not an open-source license and grants no permission
to copy, modify, or distribute QC Remote. Third-party components remain subject
to their own licenses and notices.
