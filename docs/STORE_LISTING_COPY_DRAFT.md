# QC Remote store-listing copy draft

Prepared 2026-09-07. This is conservative draft copy for future store listings,
not a completed trademark clearance or legal opinion. Publisher identity,
contacts, policy URLs, supported markets, and the final name-clearance decision
remain release blockers in `legal/public-release.json`.

## Name and subtitle

- **Name:** QC Remote
- **Subtitle:** Independent controller for compatible guitar hardware

Do not use `Quad Cortex`, `Neural DSP`, `CorOS`, or a confusing abbreviation of
them as the developer/publisher name, icon text, or leading store title. Use the
full product name only where needed to explain compatibility.

## Short description

Independent controller for compatible guitar hardware.

## Required first-paragraph disclosure

QC Remote is an independent, unofficial application for use with compatible
Quad Cortex devices. It is not affiliated with, authorized, sponsored,
endorsed, or supported by Neural DSP Technologies Oy.

## Compatibility and trademark notice

Neural DSP and Quad Cortex are trademarks of Neural DSP Technologies Oy. Those
names are used only to identify compatibility. All other trademarks belong to
their respective owners.

## Claims to avoid

- Do not call the app official, approved, certified, partnered, or supported by
  Neural DSP unless written authorization actually exists.
- Do not use Neural DSP's logo, the Quad Cortex wordmark, copied product art,
  CorOS screenshots, manual artwork, or near-identical trade dress in the icon,
  screenshots, feature graphic, advertising, or website.
- Do not imply that every firmware version, model, preset, cloud feature, or
  device operation is guaranteed to work.
- Do not claim that model-provider access, subscriptions, media rights, or
  third-party services are included unless the released configuration and terms
  prove that claim.
- Do not publish screenshots captured from a physical device or its manual.
  Release screenshots must show only QC Remote's independently styled UI and
  user-owned or clearly licensed content.

## Release-copy checklist

- Put the non-affiliation disclosure in the first visible description section,
  not only behind an About or Legal link.
- Use the final legal publisher name and support contact from
  `legal/public-release.json`.
- Link the final HTTPS privacy policy and terms.
- Accurately disclose cloud model processing, attachments, microphone use,
  connected-device access, relay behavior, retention, and deletion.
- Recheck the listing, screenshots, localization, package identifier, developer
  name, domain, and advertising together during final name and visual clearance.
