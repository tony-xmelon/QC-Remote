# Public store-listing rules

These rules are a release control, not a trademark clearance or legal opinion.
They apply to every store page, download page, installer description, screenshot,
video, social profile, and support page for QC Remote.

## Required presentation

- Publisher identity must be the actual person or legal entity distributing the
  build. Do not use Neural DSP, Quad Cortex, CorOS, or Cortex as the publisher.
- Show `Independent and unofficial` adjacent to the product description; do not
  hide it only in a privacy policy or expandable section.
- Use this compatibility sentence: `Works with compatible Quad Cortex devices.
  Not affiliated with or endorsed by Neural DSP Technologies Oy.`
- Refer to `Quad Cortex`, `Neural DSP`, and `CorOS` only in prose needed to
  explain compatibility. Do not use those marks in the app icon, publisher name,
  screenshot decoration, or advertising badges.
- Use only the original QC Remote launcher icon and independently created
  product screenshots. Do not use Neural DSP logos, product photography, manual
  artwork, screenshots, or store assets without written permission.
- Do not use `official`, `authorized`, `certified`, `native`, or similar wording
  that could imply approval. Statements such as `identical to the device` and
  `the official remote` are prohibited.
- Describe device support as firmware-sensitive and do not promise compatibility
  with future firmware.

## Name hold

`QC Remote` must remain an internal/pre-release name until the name-clearance
item in `RELEASE_LEGAL_IP_REVIEW.md` is resolved. A preliminary search found an
existing audio-industry product called `QC Remote Configuration Software` from
KLIPPEL. Store accounts, domains, social handles, and paid promotion must not be
purchased under this name merely because a basic web or app-store search appears
clear.

## Release evidence

Before a public release, archive:

1. The final listing text and every screenshot for each store and country.
2. The publisher's legal name and support/takedown contact.
3. The completed name-search record and decision.
4. Provenance for every visual and media asset in the listing.
5. The exact binary's third-party notices, privacy policy version, and terms.
